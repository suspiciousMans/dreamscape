# Stream HANDSCORE (branch feat/handscore, worktree /home/_l/dreamscape-gaps/handscore, CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/home/_l/dreamscape-gaps/target-handscore)
The last gambling-flavoured idea: your 5-card hand scores like a poker hand, Balatro-style, and the score multiplies your dust.
1. New pure `handscore.rs`: `classify(hand: &[HandFace]) -> HandRank` over what is IN the hand right now. `HandFace` = (attribute, rarity, active ability, foil) taken from booklet cards (tool cards are wild: they count as any one attribute that helps, once per hand). Ranks (name, multiplier consts, all `const`): NONE x1.00; PAIR (2 same attribute) x1.10; TWO PAIR x1.20; TRIO x1.30; FULL HOUSE (trio+pair) x1.45; QUAD x1.60; FIVE OF A KIND (all 5 same attribute: needs wilds or a quad+wild) x1.80; FLUSH (all 5 same rarity) x1.50; RAINBOW (5 different abilities) x1.35; RESONANT PAIR (a card and its resonant partner both in hand, `fusion::is_resonant`) x1.40. Take the single best multiplier (they do not stack), hard cap `SCORE_MAX_MULT = 1.80`. Hands with fewer than 5 cards (small decks) score only on what exists; never below x1.00 (no penalties, ever).
2. **Effect:** the multiplier scales dust earned (enemy loot, calm motes' dust, the run-end dust payout via `store::run_dust` caller) at the moment it is earned, using the hand as it stands THEN; it does NOT touch the banked stash, prices, odds or anything the lottery/coin/tickets use (no compounding with the gambling). Rounding: round half up, at least +1 when the base is >=1 and rank != NONE... (document your rule; test it).
3. HUD: a small plain tag above the hand: the rank name and "x1.30" (nothing flashing; a soft pulse when the rank CHANGES, off with reduced motion). New draw/discard animation events recompute it. A line in the deck builder: "best hand with this deck: <rank>" computed by a pure best-case search over the deck (any 5 cards, tool cards excluded) so players can build toward it (cheap: C(12,5)=792).
4. Persist `best_hand` rank in the booklet (`#[serde(default)]`) and show it in the summary screen line ("best hand: FULL HOUSE"); one new achievement `full_house` ("Full House": hold a full house or better) following achievements.rs conventions (ACHIEVEMENTS.md wave 3, Steam id = api name). Co-op, autopilot, fixed-loadout runs: inert (x1.00, tag hidden).
5. Tests: every rank with hand-built faces, tie to the best single multiplier, wild handling, cap, small hands, the dust effect through the earning fns, deck-builder best-hand, old-save load, `audit_text_fits_every_screen`, `screen_smoke`.
