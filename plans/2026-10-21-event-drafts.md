# Between-dream events: draft text for approval

Nothing here is in the game yet. Edit freely; I'll only wire in what you approve.

Voice: short, dry, a little off, matching the dream vocab ("LEFT A VOICEMAIL",
"has cracked, quietly"). Prompts stay under ~60 characters so they fit the
pixel UI; choices under ~30. Outcomes use only things the game already has
(upgrades, curses, shards, dust, a gilded next dream), so nothing new needs
balancing from scratch.

Each choice lists its outcome in brackets. A "decline" option is always there
so an event is never a forced gamble.

---

### 1. THE HUMMING DOOR
> A door stands in the fog, humming one note. It isn't locked.

- **Open it** — [a random upgrade, or a random curse; 50/50]
- **Knock first** — [a random upgrade, no curse; costs 8 dust]
- **Walk past** — [nothing]

### 2. THE VOICE THAT CARRIES
> A voice offers to carry something for you.

- **Give it a card** — [lose one held upgrade; +1 shard next dream; next dream gilded]
- **Give it dust** — [lose 15 dust; next dream gilded]
- **Say you're fine** — [nothing]

### 3. THE EMPTY CHAIR
> Someone left a chair here. It's still warm.

- **Sit** — [heal nothing here, so: reroll the next upgrade pick for free]
- **Take the chair** — [+10 dust]
- **Leave it** — [nothing]

### 4. THE LOST AND FOUND
> A box of things people forgot they dreamed.

- **Dig through it** — [a curse is added; in return, choose 1 of 3 upgrades]
- **Take the top item** — [a random common upgrade]
- **Close the lid** — [nothing]

### 5. THE SECOND MOON
> There are two moons tonight. One of them blinks.

- **Wave at the blinking one** — [next dream gets a random twist, but pays double dust]
- **Wave at the steady one** — [+1 sight for the rest of this run; no dust bonus]
- **Don't look up** — [nothing]

---

## Notes for you to decide

- Event 3 is deliberately small. If you'd rather every event carry some risk,
  I can swap "Take the chair" for a lose-dust option.
- Event 5's "+1 sight" and "random twist" are the only ones that touch
  gameplay rules rather than currencies. I'd playtest those two first.
- Rate: about one dream in six, never at depth 1, never right after another
  event, never in the Awakening.
- Five is a small pool for a long run. I'd expect to want ~12 eventually.
  Say the word and I'll draft more in the same voice.
- Any line you rewrite, I'll keep verbatim.
